How to use TatkalEverything on this screen, in the order you would show it
The screen
  • Top strip: the eight cases (S1 to S8). Click one, or press its number.
  • What Tatkal is doing: each step lights up as the agent works.
  • Left phone: the customer. Right box: the shopkeeper's Soundbox.
  • Middle: the ruling, the RBI clock and the action gate. Nothing is sent to NPCI until every check is green.
  • Bottom: the audit chain and the Evaluation tab.
Talking
  • Tap a quick reply, type, or press 🎙 and speak (tap again to stop).
  • A spoken yes is read back; answer "haan, confirm".
  • ▶ play on any message speaks it with Bulbul. The first sentence starts while the rest load.
Showing it
  1. ▶ Play demo: hands free, captioned, about 2 minutes.
  2. Or by hand: S4 → "Haan… lekin" → "Haan, de diya" → reopen S4 → "Nahi diya" → "Refund kar diya" → S8 → S7.
  3. 📱 Judges: QR code; their phone becomes the Soundbox.
  4. 🪔 Rush: 1 lakh synthetic cases through the real engine.
  5. Audit tab → Simulate an insider edit.
